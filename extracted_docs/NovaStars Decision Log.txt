
NovaStars Decision Log (Architecture Decision Records)
Version: 1.0 (Frozen)
Status: Canonical Decision Log
Purpose: Record the most important product, learning, gameplay and engineering decisions behind NovaStars so future team members and AI agents understand not only what was built, but why.

ADR-001 — Competency is the Product
Status
Accepted
Decision
NovaStars is a competency platform.
Lessons are not the product.
Competency development is the product.
Rationale
Children do not benefit from completing lessons.
They benefit from developing real-world competencies.
Every engineering decision must ultimately support competency development rather than lesson completion.
Consequences
Success metrics focus on competency evidence rather than lesson completion.

ADR-002 — Competency-First Architecture
Status
Accepted
Decision
All content originates from Competency Packages.
Learning experiences, stories, games and assessments are generated from competencies.
Rationale
Competencies remain stable.
Content evolves.
Separating competencies from content enables continuous improvement without changing learning objectives.

ADR-003 — Story Before Instruction
Status
Accepted
Decision
Every lesson begins with a story.
Instruction is embedded inside gameplay.
Rationale
Children engage emotionally with stories before abstract instruction.
Story creates motivation.
Gameplay creates practice.
Reflection creates meaning.

ADR-004 — Gameplay Before Infrastructure
Status
Accepted
Decision
Engineering always prioritizes playable experiences over infrastructure completeness.
Rationale
The fastest way to validate product assumptions is through playable software.
Infrastructure exists to support gameplay.

ADR-005 — Vertical Slice Development
Status
Accepted
Decision
Development proceeds through complete playable slices rather than horizontal technical layers.
Rationale
Every milestone should produce something children can actually play.

ADR-006 — Content-Driven Architecture
Status
Accepted
Decision
All lesson content is loaded from Content Packages.
Widgets never contain learning content.
Rationale
Separating content from code enables rapid iteration and future AI content generation.

ADR-007 — Configuration Over Hardcoding
Status
Accepted
Decision
Gameplay parameters belong in GameConfig rather than application code.
Examples include:
Rewards
XP
Animation timing
Difficulty
Unlock rules
Rationale
Designers should be able to tune gameplay without modifying code.

ADR-008 — MVP Before Scale
Status
Accepted
Decision
The MVP includes only systems required to validate the core learning loop.
Advanced systems such as AI tutoring, multiplayer, live events and CMS are intentionally deferred.
Rationale
Focus accelerates learning.
Premature scalability delays validation.

ADR-009 — Guest First
Status
Accepted
Decision
Guest Login is the primary authentication mechanism for MVP.
Rationale
Reducing friction increases the likelihood that children begin playing immediately.

ADR-010 — Evidence Before Mastery
Status
Accepted
Decision
Competency mastery requires real-world evidence.
Completing a lesson alone is insufficient.
Validation Flow
Lesson
↓
Real-life Challenge
↓
Parent Confirmation
↓
Competency Evidence
↓
Competency Mastered

ADR-011 — Parent as Learning Partner
Status
Accepted
Decision
Parents participate only at key moments that validate real-world behavior.
They are not expected to manage daily learning.
Rationale
Parental involvement should reinforce transfer, not interrupt gameplay.

ADR-012 — Positive Failure
Status
Accepted
Decision
Failure never punishes.
Failure teaches.
Rationale
Children should always feel encouraged to try again.
Mistakes are part of learning.

ADR-013 — Reward Growth, Not Only Correctness
Status
Accepted
Decision
Rewards recognize effort, persistence and real-world application, not only perfect answers.
Examples
Completing a challenge
Trying again
Maintaining a streak
Practicing outside the app

ADR-014 — Replay Must Have Purpose
Status
Accepted
Decision
Replay exists to deepen mastery rather than repeat content.
Motivation
Better score
Better mastery
Improved confidence
Different story outcomes

ADR-015 — AI Is an Enhancement, Not the Foundation
Status
Accepted
Decision
The MVP functions without AI.
AI enhances future versions through:
Adaptive hints
Roleplay
Coaching
Personalization
Rationale
Core gameplay must remain valuable even without AI.

ADR-016 — Feature-First + Gameplay-First Workspace
Status
Accepted
Decision
Engineering modules are organized by feature while preserving the gameplay flow.
Rationale
This balances Flutter maintainability with game production needs.

ADR-017 — Playtest Over Opinion
Status
Accepted
Decision
Real user behavior outweighs internal discussion.
Rationale
When product opinions conflict, observe children.
Children decide.

ADR-018 — Every Milestone Must Be Playable
Status
Accepted
Decision
No milestone is considered complete until it can be played by a real child.
Engineering completion alone is insufficient.

ADR-019 — Freeze Before Build
Status
Accepted
Decision
Vision, Competency Framework, Learning Framework, Roadmap, Workspace and Implementation Plan are frozen before implementation.
Rationale
Engineering should execute, not continuously redesign.

ADR-020 — Build for Tomorrow Without Delaying Today
Status
Accepted
Decision
The architecture should allow future expansion without implementing future systems prematurely.
Future Extension Points
AI Content Factory
Remote Config
Cloud Sync
Multiplayer
Seasonal Events
Teacher Dashboard
Parent Dashboard
Additional Worlds

How to Use This Decision Log
Before introducing a major product or engineering change:
Check whether an ADR already exists.
If it exists, follow it unless there is strong evidence to change.
If a change is required, create a new ADR rather than silently changing an old one.
Never overwrite historical decisions. Mark them as Superseded if they are replaced.
This document preserves the reasoning behind NovaStars and ensures long-term consistency as the product, team and AI production system evolve.
