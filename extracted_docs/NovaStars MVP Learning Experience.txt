
NovaStars MVP Learning Experience (Executive Summary)
Purpose
This document defines the standard learning journey for every competency in the NovaStars MVP.
Every competency follows the same structure to ensure a consistent, engaging and measurable learning experience.
The objective is not only to help children answer correctly inside the app, but to help them demonstrate the competency in real life.

Learning Philosophy
NovaStars follows five principles:
Learn by experiencing.
Learn through stories before instruction.
Practice through play.
Apply in real life.
Reinforce success with positive feedback.
The lesson is designed to build confidence rather than create pressure.

Learning Stages
Stage 1 — Pre-test
Goal
Quickly estimate whether the child already masters the competency.
Characteristics
Short assessment.
Situation-based questions.
Immediate feedback.
No punishment for wrong answers.
Outcomes
≥90% → Competency mastered → Skip to Reward.
<90% → Continue to learning journey.

Stage 2 — Story
Goal
Create emotional engagement before teaching.
Characteristics
Story-driven.
Branching decisions.
Child identifies with the main character.
NPCs appear when needed.
Nova characters (Su, Kem, Sao Nova) accompany the learner.
Success
The child understands why the competency matters.

Stage 3 — Mini Games
Goal
Practice the competency through interaction.
Structure
Three short mini games.
Each game trains a different aspect of the competency.
Examples:
Drag & Drop
Decision Making
Matching
Sequencing
Simulation
Conversation Practice
Rules
Positive feedback.
Unlimited retries.
Wrong answers trigger hints.
Difficulty remains constant.

Stage 4 — Boss Battle
Goal
Demonstrate competency in a complete scenario.
Characteristics
Story-based challenge.
One integrated situation.
Multiple decisions.
No memorization.
Can be replayed.
Success
The child successfully solves the problem using the competency.

Stage 5 — Reflection
Goal
Help children consolidate learning.
Reflection prompts
What happened?
What did you learn?
What would you do next time?
Why was that a good choice?
Reflection happens inside the app.

Stage 6 — Real-life Challenge
Goal
Transfer learning into daily life.
Characteristics
Can be completed within one day.
Safe.
Age appropriate.
Practical.
Related directly to the competency.
Examples
Greet a new classmate.
Organize your school bag.
Refuse politely.
Help a family member.

Stage 7 — Parent Verification
Parents verify completion inside the child's device.
Verification uses:
Parent PIN / OTP.
Suggested observation criteria.
Simple confirmation.
The goal is verification, not grading.

Stage 8 — Post-test
Goal
Measure competency mastery after learning.
Pass Criteria
Score ≥90%.
Complete Boss Battle.
Complete Reflection.
Complete Real-life Challenge.
If the learner does not reach mastery, they may retry without penalty.

Stage 9 — Reward
Children receive:
XP
Stars
Coins
Badges
Avatar Items
Pets
Island Progress
Unlocks
Rewards celebrate effort, persistence and competency mastery.

AI Support
AI assistance is available only when the learner requests Help.
AI may:
Explain mistakes.
Give hints.
Encourage the learner.
Generate equivalent practice questions.
Role-play conversations.
AI never gives the answer directly.

Success Criteria
A learning experience is successful when the learner:
Understands why the competency matters.
Successfully completes the learning journey.
Demonstrates the competency in the Boss Battle.
Applies the competency in a real-life challenge.
Passes the Post-test with at least 90%.
Feels more confident than before starting.

Design Rules
Every lesson must:
Teach exactly one competency.
Have one clear learning objective.
Include one memorable story.
Include three meaningful mini games.
Include one Boss Battle.
End with one Reflection.
Include one real-life challenge.
Finish with positive reinforcement.

Non-Negotiable Principles
Competency before knowledge.
Story before explanation.
Play before instruction.
Experience before memorization.
Safe failure.
Positive reinforcement.
One lesson = One competency.
Every lesson ends with a sense of achievement.
Every lesson creates one "Aha!" moment.
Every lesson prepares children to act confidently in real life.

Definition of Done
A Learning Experience is considered complete only when:
The competency objective has been taught.
The behavior objective has been practiced.
The learner has completed every stage.
The learner has demonstrated competency in the Boss Battle.
The learner has completed the real-life challenge.
Parent verification has been submitted.
The learner has passed the Post-test.
Rewards have been granted.
Progress has been saved.
