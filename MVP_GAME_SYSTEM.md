# NovaStars MVP Game System (Executive Summary)

## XP
- Earn XP by completing lessons.
- Bonus XP for persistence, Daily Quest and real-life challenges.
- XP represents learning progress.

---

## Stars
- Stars measure competency mastery.
- Earn Stars by completing lessons and Boss Battles.
- Stars unlock new content.

---

## Unlock
Children unlock:
- New lessons
- New islands
- New worlds
- Rewards
- Cosmetics

Unlocks happen through Stars or premium purchase.

---

## Progress
Progress is shown through:

- Lesson completion
- Island completion
- Competency completion
- World completion

Children always know where they are and what comes next.

---

## World Map
The app uses an adventure world map.

- Every World represents a major competency domain.
- Worlds contain multiple islands.
- Players freely explore unlocked areas.

---

## Islands
Each Island represents one competency group.

Each island contains:

- Multiple competencies
- Boss Battle
- Completion reward

Completing an island unlocks the next island.

---

## Boss
Each competency ends with one Boss Battle.

Purpose:

- Validate mastery
- Apply learning in scenarios
- Create memorable achievement

Bosses can be replayed.

---

## Daily Quest
Every day children receive simple quests:

- Complete one lesson
- Practice one competency
- Finish one real-life challenge

Daily Quests build learning habits.

---

## Reward
Rewards include:

- XP
- Stars
- Coins
- Badges
- Pets
- Skins
- Avatar items
- Island decorations
- Unlocks

Rewards celebrate effort, persistence and real-world behavior.

---

## Design Principles

- Adventure First
- Competency First
- Positive Reinforcement
- Safe Failure
- Progress is Visible
- One Competency at a Time
