# NovaStars MVP Context — Product Foundation (Executive Summary)

## 1. Vision

NovaStars is a Duolingo-inspired competency learning platform for primary school students.

Our vision is to become the world's leading competency development platform where children do not simply learn knowledge, but develop real-world behaviors, confidence and lifelong life skills through play.

Every child should leave the platform more capable in real life than before entering it.

---

## 2. Target Users

### Primary Users

Children aged **6–11 years old** (Primary School).

Characteristics:

- Love games and adventure.
- Learn best through stories and interaction.
- Short attention span.
- Motivated by progress, rewards and discovery.
- Want to feel competent rather than tested.

### Secondary Users

Parents.

Role:

- Companion.
- Behavior verifier.
- Progress observer.
- Motivation supporter.

Parents are not teachers.

Parents only help connect learning inside the app with real life.

---

## 3. Core Value Proposition

NovaStars transforms screen time into real-life competency development.

Instead of teaching children what to know, NovaStars helps children become someone who can act confidently in real situations.

The product combines:

- Interactive Stories
- Mini Games
- Decision Making
- Reflection
- Real-life Challenges
- Parent Verification

to create genuine competency growth.

---

## 4. North Star

Our ultimate goal is not lesson completion.

Our ultimate goal is **real-world competency mastery**.

Every design decision should answer one question:

> "Will this help children become more capable outside the app?"

Success is measured by real behavior change rather than quiz scores.

---

## 5. Core Learning Loop

```text
Select Competency
        ↓
Pre-Test
        ↓
Pass?
   ↓           ↓
Yes           No
 ↓             ↓
Reward      Story
               ↓
         Mini Games
               ↓
         Boss Battle
               ↓
          Reflection
               ↓
     Real-life Challenge
               ↓
 Parent Verification
               ↓
          Post-Test
               ↓
      Competency Mastery
               ↓
      Rewards & Progress
```

Children should always understand:

- Where they are.
- Why they are learning.
- What they should do next.
- What they will earn.

---

## 6. MVP Goal

The MVP exists to validate one hypothesis:

> **Primary school students are willing to return every day to learn life skills when the experience feels like an adventure game rather than a traditional lesson.**

The MVP is successful if it proves:

- Children enjoy the experience.
- Children complete a full competency journey.
- Children want to start another competency.
- Parents understand the educational value.
- One complete learning loop works from beginning to end.

The MVP does NOT attempt to validate:

- Large-scale AI production.
- CMS.
- Enterprise architecture.
- Advanced analytics.
- Multi-country deployment.

The only objective is to validate the core NovaStars learning experience.

---

# Product Principles

- Competency First.
- Story Before Instruction.
- Play Before Teaching.
- Experience Before Explanation.
- Positive Reinforcement.
- Safe Failure.
- One Competency Per Lesson.
- Real-life Application Matters More Than Quiz Scores.
- Human Verification Builds Trust.
- Simplicity Over Complexity.

---

# Definition of Success

A child can:

- Open the app.
- Choose an island.
- Complete one competency.
- Feel more confident.
- Want to continue learning tomorrow.

If this happens consistently, the MVP has achieved its purpose.
